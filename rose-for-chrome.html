Good to know

Before you try Rose.

An honest look at what the current Chrome preview does and needs.

Is Rose in the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. The current preview is installed locally as an unpacked Chrome extension and needs a local companion. We’ll explain the setup to invited testers.

Will it work on every website?

Rose can help on supported websites you open in Chrome. Chrome's internal pages are restricted, and some third-party sites or unusual layouts may not provide enough information for reliable guidance.

Does Rose click or submit things for me?

No. Rose can point out a suggested control or answer, but it does not select answers, fill forms, submit requests, or make purchases for you.

How is the preview connected?

The preview uses a local companion and a provider you choose. Its ChatGPT-labeled option uses a locally signed-in Codex CLI, rather than a direct browser sign-in. OpenAI and Anthropic API-key options are also available; provider usage may be billed separately.
