Good to know

A few honest answers.

Rose for Chrome is in preview. Here's what to expect today.

Can I install Rose from the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. The current build is a private, unpacked Chrome preview that also needs a local companion. Ask for early access and we'll explain the setup and requirements.

Does Rose click buttons or fill forms for me?

Rose can guide you to controls and highlight a suggested next step. It does not select answers, fill or submit forms, or complete a purchase on your behalf.

Can I use it on every website?

Rose works on supported websites you open in Chrome. Chrome's own internal pages are restricted, and some sites or page layouts may not offer enough information for a reliable guide.

How does Rose answer general questions?

The preview uses a local companion with a connected provider. The current ChatGPT-labeled option uses a locally signed-in Codex CLI; OpenAI and Anthropic API-key options are also available. API-key usage may have separate provider charges.
