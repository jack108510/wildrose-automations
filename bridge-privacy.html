Bridge privacy notice

Updated November 23, 2026 · Limited pilot

This notice covers Wildrose Bridge, the limited WebMCP connector pilot operated by Wildrose Automations. Bridge is separate from Rose. Questions and requests can be sent to support@wildroseautomations.ca.

Applications and enquiries

Online applications are currently closed. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your enquiry, assess pilot fit and arrange access. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information. Email is also handled by the email providers involved.

Information used by the connector

Access is limited to operator-created accounts. The service stores account identifiers, a password hash, assistant client registrations and authorization records. Access and refresh tokens support your connection to the assistant. Browser cookies and site storage are kept in an isolated browser context for your account while its session is active.

The assistant sends the website address, tool name and arguments needed for an operation to Bridge. Bridge opens the site, passes the requested arguments to its published tool and returns the response to your assistant. Websites receive the requests made to them. Your assistant provider and the websites you use handle information under their own policies.

Operational records

Bridge's tool audit records include the time, a pseudonymous account identifier, operation, site origin, outcome and whether confirmation was supplied. These audit records omit conversation text, tool arguments and site response content. Network and hosting providers may process connection information such as IP addresses and request metadata.

Action execution records also retain hashed operation and approval fingerprints and execution status to prevent repeat attempts when an outcome is uncertain. These records do not retain raw tool arguments or website response bodies. Execution status alone is not independent proof that a booking or purchase was completed.

Hosting and retention

Bridge runs on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States (us-west1). Account, authorization and action execution records persist across restarts. Daily copies of that state are kept on the service's data disk for approximately one week. Browser tabs do not survive a service restart. Logs, account records and enquiries may remain until removed through operational maintenance or a deletion request; this pilot does not promise a fixed automatic deletion schedule for those records.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in is not enabled yet. If enabled later, it will request your Google account identifier and verified email using the openid and email scopes to match an existing pilot account. The implementation does not retain Google access or refresh tokens. This notice and the sign-in screen must reflect the enabled configuration before that option opens.

Your choices

You can remove Bridge from your assistant's integrations at any time. To request access, correction, account revocation or deletion of account and session data, contact support from the address associated with your pilot account. We may need to verify your identity. Removing a connector from the assistant does not itself erase server records, previous assistant responses or copies held by third-party websites. Deleted live records may remain in existing backups until those copies expire.

Changes

We will update this notice as the pilot changes and explain material changes to collection or use before asking you to continue under them.
