Questions

Good questions.

Really no setup fee?+

Really. $5.99/mo for a Basic Site includes everything — design, build, hosting, SSL. The only time there's an upfront cost is custom app development, and those are quoted upfront with no surprises.

What if I want to leave?+

Cancel anytime, no penalty. You own your domain. I'll help you migrate wherever you want to go. No lock-in, no hard feelings.

How fast can you build it?+

First draft within 48 hours for standard sites. Most business sites are live within a week. Larger projects (apps, platforms) are scoped with a timeline upfront.

Are you really just one person?+

Yes. Every line of code, every design decision, every deployment — me. That's why it's affordable. No agency markup, no project managers, no layers between you and the person doing the work.

What about ongoing changes?+

Content updates are included on the Basic Site plan. Need a new page, a new feature, or more complex functionality? That falls under Business Site or App Development — just message me and I'll scope it.

What's the difference between Basic and Business Site?+

Basic Site ($5.99/mo) is a simple info site — contact info, services, photos. No payments, no booking, no backend. Business Site is for anything that needs functionality: scheduling, payments, custom portals, dashboards. Those are quoted based on scope.

Where are you based?+

Edmonton, Alberta. I work with clients across Canada. Everything is remote — email, phone, video calls, whatever works for you.
