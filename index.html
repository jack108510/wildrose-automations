Questions

Good questions.

Really no setup fee?+

Really. $29/mo for a Starter Site includes everything — design, build, hosting, SSL. The only time there's an upfront cost is custom app/AI projects, and those are quoted upfront with no surprises.

What if I want to leave?+

Cancel anytime, no penalty. You own your domain. I'll help you migrate wherever you want to go. No lock-in, no hard feelings.

How fast can you build it?+

First draft within 48 hours for standard sites. Most business sites are live within a week. Larger projects (apps, AI tools) are scoped with a timeline upfront.

Are you really just one person?+

Yes. Every line of code, every design decision, every deployment — me. That's why it's affordable. No agency markup, no project managers, no layers between you and the person doing the work.

What about ongoing changes?+

Text and image changes are unlimited on all plans. Need a new page, a new feature, or design changes? Just message me. Bigger changes are included on Business and Custom plans.

Where are you based?+

Edmonton, Alberta. I work with clients across Canada. Everything is remote — email, phone, video calls, whatever works for you.
