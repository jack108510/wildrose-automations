Questions

Good questions.

Is this Rose?

Rose is the virtual front desk on the Wildrose site. Your business gets its own branded front desk with your name, colors, services, and intake process.

Do I need a new website?

No. The easiest version can sit on your current site. If the site itself is hurting conversions, we can also rebuild or facelift it.

Does it confirm bookings?

Only if we connect it to a real booking system. Otherwise it captures the request clearly and tells the customer your team will confirm.

Can it use voice?

Yes, when it helps the customer. Chat is the clean default because it is low-friction and easy to launch. Voice can be added for higher-touch businesses.

What happens to leads?

They can be routed to email, SMS, CRM, calendar, or an n8n workflow. The first version usually starts with email routing, then expands.
