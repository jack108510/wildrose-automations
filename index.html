FAQ

Questions,
answered by Rose.

Something else on your mind? Email jack@wildroseautomations.ca.

Can I see Rose on my site before I pay?

Yes! Pop your website into the box at the top of this page and I’ll show up on it right here. Nothing gets installed. When you configure me, you can chat with me and talk to me out loud before you choose a plan.

Do I need to install anything myself?

Nope. After checkout, the Wildrose team gets me live. Most owners invite them as a website contributor. They can also guide you on a call, send my snippet to your web person, or set up a hosted Rose page if your builder blocks custom code.

What does Rose do when someone asks for a quote?

I ask the follow-up questions you would, collect their contact details and send the request straight to you, so you can reply while the lead is still warm.

Can I change what Rose says?

Of course. Everything from your website scan carries into Workground, where you can update my business facts, greeting, tone and colours whenever you like.

What if Rose can’t answer a question?

I hand off to you. I take the visitor’s contact details and pass their request along, so nobody leaves without a way to reach you.

What happens if I go over my voice minutes?

Extra voice minutes are CA$0.29 each. Chat is included on both plans, so I can keep typing all day.
