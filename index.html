FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Email jack@wildroseautomations.ca.

Can I see Rose on my site before I pay?

Yes. Enter your website at the top of this page and a preview appears right here. Nothing is installed. When you configure your agent you can also talk to her by chat and voice before choosing a plan.

Do I need to install anything myself?

No. After checkout, Wildrose helps get Rose live. Most owners invite us as a website contributor, or we can guide you on a call, send the snippet to your web person, or set up a hosted Rose page if your builder blocks custom code.

What does Rose do when someone asks for a quote?

She asks the right follow-up questions, collects the visitor’s contact details and sends the request to you, so you can reply while the lead is still warm.

Can I change what Rose says?

Yes. Your website scan and choices carry into Workground, where you can update business facts, her greeting, tone and colours at any time.

What if Rose can’t answer a question?

Rose offers a handoff to you: she takes the visitor’s contact details and passes the request along, so nobody leaves without a way to reach you.

What happens if I go over my voice minutes?

Extra voice minutes are CA$0.29 each. Chat is included on both plans.
