Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Wildrose Automations ("Wildrose," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, use our AI assistants, request a demo, purchase a service, communicate with us, or interact with our Meta/Facebook app and advertising tools.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Wildrose, we may collect:

  • Contact and business information, including name, business name, website URL, email address, phone number, and messages you send us.
  • Service and onboarding information, including details about your website, requested AI assistant setup, business hours, lead routing preferences, and support requests.
  • AI/chat and voice content, including messages, call transcripts, summaries, and metadata needed to operate, improve, and support our AI assistant services.
  • Payment and billing information, processed by third-party payment providers. We do not store full credit card numbers on our own servers.
  • Website, device, and usage data, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring URLs, timestamps, diagnostics, and interaction events.
  • Meta/Facebook information, if you interact with our Facebook Login, business tools, ads, pixels, or connected Meta services, subject to the permissions you grant and Meta's own policies.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide websites, AI assistants, automations, demos, support, and related services.
  • Respond to inquiries, route leads, and communicate about projects or accounts.
  • Create, configure, test, secure, and maintain customer AI tools.
  • Process payments, subscriptions, invoices, and service records.
  • Measure website performance, marketing effectiveness, and product usage.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate spam, abuse, fraud, service misuse, and security incidents.
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, platform, and regulatory obligations.

Cookies, Pixels, Analytics, and Advertising

We may use cookies, pixels, analytics scripts, and similar technologies, including Microsoft Clarity and Meta Pixel, to understand website usage, improve our services, measure advertising performance, and show relevant ads. These tools may collect information about your interactions with our website and other sites over time. You can control cookies through your browser settings and platform ad preferences.

AI Assistants, Chat, and Voice

When you use a Wildrose AI assistant, your messages, call audio-derived transcripts, summaries, and related metadata may be processed by third-party AI, telephony, hosting, and database providers so the assistant can respond, route leads, and help the business follow up. Do not submit sensitive information unless necessary for the request.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information in the traditional sense. We may share information with service providers that help us operate Wildrose, including hosting, analytics, advertising, payment, email, SMS/voice, database, AI, customer support, and security providers. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including service delivery, support, security, legal compliance, accounting, and dispute resolution. When information is no longer needed, we may delete, anonymize, or securely retain it as required by law.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No internet or electronic storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict certain uses of your information. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time. To make a privacy request, contact us at jack@wildroseautomations.ca.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13 or the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International Users

If you access our services from outside Canada, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries where privacy laws may differ from those in your location.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of our services after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to jack@wildroseautomations.ca.
