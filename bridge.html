Before you ask

A few things
to know.

Is this another AI I have to switch to?

No. Bridge connects to Claude or ChatGPT. You keep using your assistant, in your existing chat, with access to tools on supported websites.

Can it buy things or make bookings?

Real purchases, checkout and bookings are disabled in this pilot. We're starting with finding and reading information, plus controlled demonstrations.

Does it work on every website?

Not yet. A website needs to offer compatible tools. The list above distinguishes tools that have been tested from those that need more setup.

What happens to my information?

Your browser session is separate from other users. Tool audit logs omit your conversation text, tool arguments and website response content. Your assistant and the websites you use still process information under their own policies.

You can disconnect in your assistant at any time. Contact us to request account and session deletion. Read the privacy notice for storage, hosting and retention details.

How do I get access?

Contact us about the pilot and tell us what you'd like to do. Access is individually approved. We'll provide the account and connection instructions.

The branded connector address and Google sign-in aren't active yet. Use the details supplied with your invitation. Online applications are not open.

Are there limits?

The pilot currently allows one active session and three website tabs per account. Tool calls time out after 25 seconds. Some site features are blocked and availability isn't guaranteed. See the pilot terms.
