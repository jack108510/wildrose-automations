A few things to know

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Is this another AI app?

No. Bridge connects to the assistant you already use. You keep talking in Claude or ChatGPT; Bridge gives that assistant access to tools on supported websites.

Does it work on every website?

Not yet. A website needs to provide compatible tools before Bridge can use them. The examples above show what has been tested and what still needs setup.

How is this different from a web search?

Web search helps you find information. Bridge lets your assistant use a supported website's own tools, such as its catalogue search, and bring the results into your conversation.

Can it buy things or book appointments?

Those actions are disabled in the current pilot. We're starting with reading and searching, plus controlled demonstrations, before opening up more actions.

How do I connect it?

Approved pilot users receive a connector address and setup instructions for their assistant. You add Bridge in your assistant's connector settings, sign in with your pilot account, and approve the connection.

The branded connector address is not active yet. Google sign-in is also not enabled yet; use the account instructions supplied with your invitation.

What are the pilot's limits?

Access is individually approved. The service currently allows one active session and three website tabs per account, with a 25-second tool timeout. Some website features are blocked and availability is not guaranteed.
