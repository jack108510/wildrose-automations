How it works

The bridge between
conversation and action

WebMCP is a browser standard that lets websites publish tools directly to AI assistants. Wildrose Bridge runs a secure browser session on your behalf, discovers those tools in real time, and executes approved ones.

Connection instructions

Setting up
your connection

Connect your AI assistant once after your pilot access is approved. The branded connector address below is planned for launch and is not active yet. Invited pilot users should use the connector address supplied with their invitation.

MCP URL https://bridge.wildroseautomations.ca/mcp
  1. Open Claude.ai settings
    Go to claude.ai → Settings → Integrations.
  2. Add an MCP connection
    Click Add connection, paste the connector URL above, and name it "Wildrose Bridge".
  3. Log in and approve permissions
    Claude will redirect you to the bridge's login page. Use the pilot credentials we sent you, then approve the requested scope.
  4. Ask Claude to use a supported site
    Try: "Search Alo Yoga for black yoga pants." Claude will discover available tools and report back.
Note: End-to-end assistant connection testing is in progress for this pilot. Approved applicants will receive verified setup guidance before onboarding.
MCP URL https://bridge.wildroseautomations.ca/mcp
  1. Open ChatGPT settings
    Go to chatgpt.com → Settings → Integrations (or Plugins).
  2. Add an MCP server
    Look for "Add MCP" or "Connect a tool", paste the URL above, and name it "Wildrose Bridge".
  3. Authorize and approve
    ChatGPT will redirect you to the bridge login. Use your pilot credentials and approve the requested permissions.
  4. Test a site search
    Try: "Using the bridge, search Alo Yoga for black yoga pants." The bridge discovers and calls the available tools.
Note: ChatGPT's MCP integration UI is subject to change. Approved applicants will receive step-by-step guidance verified against the current ChatGPT interface.

Common questions

How do I disconnect?+

Remove the connection in your assistant's integrations settings. Your pilot account and session remain active until you log out from the bridge directly or contact support to revoke access.

Why am I getting an authentication error?+

The most common cause is an expired authorization window. Try reconnecting from your assistant's integrations settings. Make sure cookies are enabled, and try a different browser if the issue persists. Contact support if you're still stuck.

Can I use it on mobile?+

The bridge runs in the cloud, so your assistant can reach it from any device. The login and consent pages are mobile-compatible. Some assistants' mobile apps may not yet support MCP connections — check your assistant's mobile release notes.

Trust & privacy

What we store,
and what we don't

The pilot is small and deliberately constrained. Here's exactly what happens inside the bridge.

🔐
Isolated sessions
Each account gets its own browser context. Cookies and storage are not shared between accounts. One active session per account.
🚫
Read-only pilot
Cart, checkout, and booking mutations are disabled. Write tools require explicit confirmation per call. Failed mutations are never retried automatically.
📋
Minimal logging
We log each tool call with its site, outcome, and timestamp. We do not log your conversation, tool arguments, or the content of site responses.
🗑
Access revocation
Remove the bridge from your assistant's integrations at any time. Contact support to delete your account and session data permanently.
Known service limits (pilot)
  • One active session and three site tabs at a time
  • Browser tool timeout: 25 seconds per call
  • Not all websites support WebMCP — the bridge can only use tools that sites publish
  • Service workers and WebSockets from sites are blocked in this build
  • No availability SLA; this is a staged pilot, not a production service
