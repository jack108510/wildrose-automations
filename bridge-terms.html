Bridge pilot terms

Updated November 23, 2026 · Limited pilot

These terms describe participation in the Wildrose Bridge pilot operated by Wildrose Automations. Bridge is a separate product from Rose. Contact support@wildroseautomations.ca about access or these terms.

Access to the pilot

Applying or making an enquiry does not create an account or guarantee admission. Access is provided individually by the operator. Online applications are currently closed. Keep your account credentials private and use only accounts, websites and data you are authorized to access.

What Bridge does

Bridge connects an AI assistant to tools a website publishes through WebMCP. A website appearing in the directory does not guarantee that every tool works. A tool may be detected without a successful call, may need configuration, or may be blocked by the pilot's restrictions. Rediscover tools on the current page before relying on them.

Permitted pilot use

The private pilot supports native website reads and user-confirmed actions when a website exposes functioning tools. Actions require explicit approval of the site, operation and exact arguments. Controlled tests and a harmless demo action have passed; real merchant bookings and purchases have not been verified end to end. A successful tool response alone does not independently verify a completed business transaction. Do not use Bridge to bypass access controls, abuse another service, collect information without authorization or make transactions you have not explicitly approved. Follow the websites' terms and the assistant provider's rules.

Limits and availability

The current host admits one active browser owner at a time and up to three site tabs. Sessions expire after 30 idle minutes. Browser tool execution has a 25-second timeout. Site service workers and WebSockets are blocked. The pilot may be interrupted, changed or paused, and has no availability SLA. These constraints may prevent otherwise valid site tools from working.

Check results before relying on them

Website content and tool responses can be incomplete, out of date or misleading. Verify consequential information with its source. The pilot is for evaluation and should not be relied on for emergency services or time-critical transactions. A failed action must not be assumed to have succeeded or be retried automatically.

Privacy and ending participation

The Bridge privacy notice explains the information processed by the pilot. Do not submit passwords, payment information or sensitive personal data in an enquiry or tool argument. You may stop using Bridge and remove it from your assistant at any time. Contact support to request account revocation or deletion. We may suspend access to protect the service or investigate misuse.

Changes and charges

These pilot terms do not authorize a purchase, subscription or future charge. Any paid offering requires a separate agreement. Material changes to pilot participation will be explained before continued participation is requested. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
