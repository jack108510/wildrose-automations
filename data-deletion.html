User Data Deletion

Effective date: August 18, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of personal information associated with Wildrose Automations, including information connected to our Meta/Facebook app, website, AI assistants, forms, and customer communications.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email jack@wildroseautomations.ca with the subject line Wildrose data deletion request.
  2. Include the email address, business name, Facebook/Meta profile name, or other identifier you used with Wildrose so we can find the relevant records.
  3. Tell us whether you want deletion from our website/app records, AI assistant records, Meta/Facebook app records, or all Wildrose systems where deletion is legally permitted.

Processing time: We will review and respond to deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe, generally within 30 days, unless a shorter period is required by applicable law.

What We Delete

When deletion is requested and verified, we will delete or anonymize personal information that we are not required to retain, which may include contact details, form submissions, chat or voice assistant records, support messages, and Meta/Facebook app-related identifiers.

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited information where necessary for legal, tax, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security, or legitimate business recordkeeping obligations. Retained information will be limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

Meta/Facebook App Data

If you connected to Wildrose through Facebook Login or another Meta service, you may also remove Wildrose's access from your Facebook settings. In Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites, then remove Wildrose Automations if it appears there. You can still email us using the instructions above to request deletion of related data in Wildrose systems.

Contact

For any data deletion or privacy request, contact jack@wildroseautomations.ca.
