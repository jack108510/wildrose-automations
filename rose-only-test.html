The system

A simple intake loop, not a giant automation project.

Rose only needs your business memory, the website widget, and a clear handoff path. That keeps it easy to sell, easy to install, and easy for the business to understand.

The point is not to impress people with AI. The point is to make sure a real lead does not leave because nobody answered fast enough.
