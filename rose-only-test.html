How Rose works

A simple loop, not a giant automation project.

Rose only needs three things to become useful: what your business does, what questions visitors ask, and where leads should go.

The point is not to impress people with AI. The point is to make sure a real lead does not leave because nobody answered fast enough.

01

Train the business memory

Services, hours, areas served, FAQs, booking links, limits, and the tone Rose should use.

02

Install the website widget

Rose appears on the site as chat and optional voice. Mobile visitors can use it without downloading anything.

03

Capture the visitor’s intent

Rose answers, then collects what matters: contact info, request type, urgency, location, and notes.

04

Send the lead packet

You get a clean summary so you can call, quote, book, or route it into your process.
